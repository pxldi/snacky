## Design specificity

Generic. system-ui (Roboto on Android), a stock sage accent, uppercase muted h2s, bordered cards; rename Protein to Schritte and it is a step counter. Nothing refers to lifting, plants, the four-meal plan or Clanky; the wordmark is 16px body text. Detector: CLI scan of templates returned 0 findings (it likely never resolved the root-absolute CSS); the in-page detector found overused-font on every page, line-length on the footer at desktop width, and "cyan neon on dark" on the week's Training tags.

## Priority issues

1. [P1] The peak is inverted: reaching the protein goal gets the same grey-to-green bar and 14px "Erreicht" as landing inside the kcal band, and on a near-miss day the only accent on screen sits on the secondary kcal card. Fix: reserve the accent for protein, make the met state a real moment, keep kcal neutral.
2. [P1] Stale undo: /?undo=<id> survives reload and back, shows "Eingetragen: X" for any id and posts a delete. Fix: undo only for UI entries under two minutes old, strip the query with history.replaceState, role="status".
3. [P1] Bland against an explicit brief ("cool, not bland"). Fix: a self-hosted display face for wordmark and hero number, a signature colour, empty states in Clanky's voice.
4. [P2] Meal distribution invisible: meals show protein but no reference unless SNACKY_MEAL_PROTEIN_MIN is set. Fix: default reference, slots under the hero filled per meal that reaches it.
5. [P2] The week does not say how the week went: future days read "0 g" like missed ones, no today marker, met vs unmet bars differ by hue only (1.08:1 / 1.15:1). Fix: headline "X von 7 Tagen", future days as "–", a mark plus text on met rows.

## Minor observations

"250 g" wraps; the kcal range breaks as "2.200–/2.600" at 390px; "1 Portion(en)"; missing fibre shows "– g"; the estimate hint counts kcal share where protein share matters; Training tags reuse the met accent; /quick cannot log its own items; every page ends on the licence line.
