## Design specificity

Authored. The day is a food pack: the front fills with protein to a dashed goal line, the back is a Nährwerttabelle with a heavy rule and hairlines, quick items are tear-off coupons, the gap sits in a burst sticker. The concept thins on the secondary pages (quick, edit, delete, error are white panels on pink) and the copy stays dry apart from "Los geht's" and "Geschafft!". Detector: templates and CSS 1 advisory before DESIGN.md existed (coupon perforation, earned), 29 advisories after (8 design-system-color template false positives, 20 design-system-font-size where CSS sizes miss the DESIGN.md ramp). URL mode: a sticker line-box overlap on /, a hidden-details false positive on /quick. In-page overlay blocked by the CSP's missing wasm-unsafe-eval; snapshot route used for dark.

## Priority issues

1. [P1] The week hero scores performance: fill is met days over counted days, today counts before it ends, so a logged week at 83 to 111 g reads "0 VON 7 TAGEN GESCHAFFT" on a dark pack. Fix: fill from protein sum over goal sum, count today only once met, headline days logged and the average against the goal, rows "101 von 120 g".
2. [P1] Coupon accessible names drop the protein ("+23,4 g"), the reason the coupon exists.
3. [P2] Log confirmation is out of thumb reach and silent after the reload; move the undo bar above the bottom nav and set the document title.
4. [P2] Past days look like today; put the weekday in the cap and style past fronts.
5. [P2] Entry rows show no edit affordance; the log starts below the fold.
